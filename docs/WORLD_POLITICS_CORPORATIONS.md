# WP53b — Professional corporations

Optional fr-corporations-v1, initially pluralist. Player establishes parallel
professional bodies, then can assert an exclusive system in a fictional contested
constitutional rupture. This is not a valid legal dissolution under current French
law: see Constitution 1958 preamble and 1946 preamble paragraphs 6–7, freedom of
association and strike. Restoration of pluralism remains possible. Independent
worker opposition and employer resistance are distinct fictional indices, not
party affiliations or predictions. Aggregate production and social pressure react;
no real organisation, person or company is identified.

Ten illustrative branches. Employee and employer colleges vote autonomously; both
must reach 50% to submit a proposed branch wage index adjustment. Reports are
quarterly or annual; government may approve or return for a new vote after seven
days. Failed votes also retry. Pending reports remain pending until reviewed.
Indices are not actual pay scales, and approved wage indices are not yet linked
to the full payroll, tax and inflation accounts. Nationalisation is a recorded
request for legal and fiscal review, not a transfer or punishment of a company;
compensation, financing, property rights, litigation and firm ownership ledger
remain to implement. No automatic suppression of opposition or regime change.

Owner-only commands use domestic ETag, idempotency and global clock fence;
commands apply the following game day. Existing scenarios and saves unchanged.
