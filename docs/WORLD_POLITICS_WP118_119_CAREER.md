# WP118–119 — Career and institutional ledger (opt-in backend)

Additive migration 039, pure deterministic French rule fixture and transactional store. Separate authenticated account, character, career, mandate, office, generation and presidential series. Signed server-side institutional evidence is bound to world, day, revision, command and payload; no documentary affiliation confers powers. Trusted adapter must verify the underlying institution before signing. Session capabilities are checked under the existing world mutation fence. The complete journal is replayed on each mutation/read; receipts and SQL active-office/membership projections are verified. No public route or live alpha activation. Research mode cannot issue grants or scores. Existing v1 unchanged.

France slice: distinct presidency, PM, seven minister portfolios, 577 deputy and 348 senator seats; explicit compatible multiple portfolios only; cabinet closure, succession, interim restricted powers, renewal and series closure, Assembly dissolution/restoration, constitutional revision gate. The evidence adapter, not this reducer, must certify the actual election, appointments, consultations, legislative vote, Article 89 process and all detailed jurisdictional requirements. Constitution revisions with active mandates fail closed pending a versioned migration. Research stores aggregate institutional observations, not authority or competitive objectives.

Tests: headless domain regression and real MariaDB two-connection lock contention, rollback, idempotence, replay, session capability and projection tamper. No production migration run. Current limitations: only FR reviewed rule fixture, no independent live institution adapter, no live institutional transition adapter, no HTTP/gameplay routing, no real-candidate admission, no certified participation credit or score publication; existing legacy alpha authorization remains unchanged and must not be mixed with the new career mode. These are remaining WP118–119 integration gates, not claims of complete worldwide coverage. Full replay is a development correctness implementation; compaction/scale remains WP124.

Follow-up: canonical receipt return, consecutive-term check even across vacancy, dissolution moratorium measured from election/restoration, explicit opt-in scenario markers and a world-clock guard at intent/publication. Guard permits reaching expiry for a same-day certified transition but rejects later days; expired capabilities are denied. Legacy worlds without a career ledger remain unaffected. Opt-in scenario provisioning and real institutional adapter remain unmounted.

WP119 composition follow-up: Article 89 effective-clause replay replaces arbitrary next-digest revisions. Two explicitly modelled prospective Article 6 changes (term limit and duration) are composed, never retroactively extend a current mandate, and preserve historical grant versions. Unknown constitutional clauses fail closed. The French bridge checks committed institutional snapshots and binds their verified hash/revision to signed dissolution, installed-election restoration and constitutional revision events. Appointment/election/other succession proofs still need their own live connectors. No legacy scenario gains new authorization.

Old world routes reject career scenario markers. No public transition signing or real-candidate unlock. This is not a fully provisioned economic multiplayer world; scheduler, full gameplay action adapters and institutional election/appointment connectors remain open. Store-level MariaDB tests cover session-bound provisioning, replay, invalid token and non-executive admission.
