# WP80 — EU and euro-area spillovers of a French withdrawal

As-of 2026-11-25. Counterfactual game research, not a forecast or political
recommendation. France alone is playable initially. Every other country,
EU institution, central bank, financial institution and market aggregate
reacts within its own competence and state. Applies to all France exit
branches, including simultaneous Russia normalisation, abandoned exit
and negotiated versus abrupt operational changes.

## Observed anchors
Eurostat 2025 EU GDP EUR 18.8tn (rounded), France 15.8% at current prices.
Insee/Eurostat 2025 France 18.4% of euro area GDP in purchasing power
standards, not nominal EUR and not 2026 membership. Euro area 21 members
after Bulgaria adopted euro on 2026-01-01. EU27. European Parliament
2024–2029 initial composition 720 seats, France 81. ECB all-EU NCB
capital subscription key France 16.3575%; Eurosystem-only normalised
key France 19.7682% and Germany 26.3152% on 2026-01-01; different
denominators. No automatic ECB capital repayment or loss allocation.
French 2025 net EU budget contribution on accounting method EUR 9.5bn,
gross EUR 25.9bn and returns EUR 16.4bn outside NGEU according to French
budget documentation/Senate. Do not mix traditional own resources,
PSR-UE and Commission net methodology. ECB June 2026: euro 20.2% of
global official FX reserves Q4 2025 at Q4 2025 constant exchange rates.
These are observations with their own periods, not common live snapshots.

## Distinct membership and geography
EU membership, Eurosystem membership, euro use, single market, customs,
Schengen, SEPA, defence agreements and energy interconnection are
separate states. Article 50 notification alone does not remove French
membership or ordinary budget obligations. France is excluded only from
the European Council/Council withdrawal-specific deliberations and
decisions covered by Article 50(4). Revocation, extension, agreement
and legal withdrawal remain distinct. EU exit with continued unilateral
euro use is not Eurosystem membership. Euro-only exit while remaining
EU member requires a negotiated legal basis or a flagged rupture.
If effective withdrawal alone and no other membership change, EU26;
if Eurosystem exit too, euro area20. No automatic collapse, treaty repeal,
NATO exit or recognition change.

Use both fixed-geography historical GDP and dynamic-membership aggregates.
At constant 2025 prices/current-euro GDP observations, removing France
reduces the EU aggregate mechanically by 15.8%, leaving 84.2% of the
old aggregate BEFORE any economic responses. This is a scope change,
not a recession of the remaining countries. The PPS share cannot be
used as a current-EUR loss. Sum national real-output changes only for
a fixed cohort with consistent price bases and FX conventions. Preserve
historical records. For each country compute growth and welfare vs
the same country's no-exit counterfactual.

## Institutional consequences
On effective withdrawal recompute Council voters, eligible population,
55% member/65% population qualified-majority rules where applicable,
blocking minority minimum four for standard full Council voting, special
participating-state and reinforced majority rules separately. Withdrawal
agreement uses Article 50/238(3)(b) special QMV and Parliament consent.
Do not equate GDP or ECB capital weights with Council population votes.
Remaining Parliament seats initially 720-81=639 before a competent
reallocation; later legal decision may change total and distributions.
Recompute commissions, party groups, mandate dates, regulatory bodies
and international representation, no automatic assignment of French
national sovereignty or treaty rights to another state. Update European
defence/diplomatic coordination and location of institutional offices
through appropriate decisions, not automatic disappearance of capabilities.

## Budget and balance sheets
EU own resources, commitments and payments distinct; multiannual financial
framework, outstanding commitments, NGEU debt/guarantees, existing
withdrawal settlement obligations, grants/loans and contingent liabilities
maintained by instrument and legal status. Annual static example only:
if all comparable French gross payments and French receipts ceased,
nothing else changed and no settlement, gross funding removed 25.9bn,
spending removed 16.4bn, residual gap 9.5bn EUR. It is not a forecast,
not the withdrawal bill, and not an immediate saving to France.
Allocate any verified gap only by competent decisions: spending cuts,
new own resources, changes to contributions, agreed borrowing within
legal powers, settlement instalments, timing adjustments. No automatic
redistribution proportional to GDP without explicit model/decision.
Track each national treasury and EU budget, intergovernmental transfers
cancel on consolidation. Distinguish NGEU from ordinary net figures.
Test sum of financing adjustments equals actual residual; no double count.

Maintain ECB and national central bank balance sheets, TARGET payment
system claims/liabilities, banknotes allocation, monetary-policy holdings,
capital and reserves, collateral, cash/payment continuity. A TARGET
balance is an accounting position, not automatically a bill due at exit.
Settlement status negotiated/contested/agreed/paid; assets and liabilities
not erased, losses only once with ownership and legal decision. The
capital key is not a share of GDP or an automatic loss share.
For illustrative pure denominator renormalisation holding all other
weights fixed, German Eurosystem share would be 26.3152/(100-19.7682)*100
= approximately 32.8%; actual future keys must be decided under applicable
rules, no implication for one-person rotating policy voting.

## Transmission to remaining countries and world
Country-specific exports to France, imports, imported inputs, tourism,
FDI, cross-border workers, contracts, bank exposure by counterparty and
currency, sovereign bond holdings and maturity, collateral haircuts,
FX mismatch and hedges. Obtain Eurostat/Comext, ECB/BIS, IMF CPIS/COFER,
national statistical agencies; distinguish claims by residence, nationality,
currency and governing law. Missing exposures explicit; no invented data.
Compare direct trade shock, second-round input-output effects, substitution
and trade diversion, investment uncertainty, competitiveness, labour
mobility, energy supply, currency/asset-price contagion, sovereign
refinancing, bank capital/liquidity, inflation, fiscal stabilisers.
An export loss is not GDP loss: account for value added, imports and
inventory. Currency valuation changes must not create nominal wealth
in consolidated balance sheets. Apply rates to new/refinanced debt only.

Euro FX against USD/GBP/CHF/etc and new French currency jointly modelled
with reciprocal cross-rate consistency; euro can depreciate, appreciate
or remain stable in response to capital allocation, interest differentials,
confidence and monetary policy. Initial stress tests for EUR external
change -20/-10/0/+10/+20%, for spread shock +0/+100/+300/+500 bps
by issuer, no probabilities or calibration claims. Global reserve
portfolio holdings and valuation effects separate; EUR reserve share
does not move mechanically by France GDP share. Include spillovers
through US/UK/Swiss and other banks and investors. ECB response is
autonomous, conditional on mandate and eligibility; TPI, monetary policy,
liquidity and bank supervision are distinct tools, not guaranteed
unlimited assistance. Model ESM support as separate competent decision
with conditions and consent. See IMF financial fragmentation 2025:
local redenomination risk need not become generalised fragmentation.

## Scenario matrix and time
All operational durations 0/3/6/12/18/24/36/48 months from prior study,
independent legal exit/notification clocks. Combine with no-exit,
revocation, negotiated exit with euro use, negotiated currency exit,
operational shock, and distinct legal rupture case. Cross with EU trade
agreement breadth, banking/currency cooperation, settlement, common
external shocks and Russia normalisation (none/partial/full lawful).
Evaluate 1/3/6/12/24/36/60/120 months from announcement and legal event.
Market anticipation may precede withdrawal, but legal rights change
only on certified event. Long transition can reduce operational stress
or extend uncertainty. No forced monotonically improving trajectory.

Autonomous paths:
A. EU continues and absorbs departure; euro remains in use by remaining
members; funding adjustment, bilateral trade arrangements, contained stress.
B. Continued EU and euro with financial fragmentation; country-specific
spread and banking pressure, slower growth or trade reallocation,
conditional central bank/institutional stabilisation.
C. Reform/integration or differentiated cooperation: remaining members
negotiate common instruments, institutional changes and funding reforms;
adoption only through valid authority.
D. Political contagion: additional governments debate reform or exit,
elections/referenda and constitutional requirements resolved independently.
No forced departure of Italy, Germany or other states after France.
E. Partial monetary fragmentation or eventual dissolution in a genuinely
multi-state crisis; only as a distinct set of legal/contractual transitions
and independent decisions. No single confidence threshold automatically
destroys the euro. Currency liabilities and contracts persist until
lawfully resolved. EU can continue even if monetary arrangement changes.
F. Reversal/renegotiation: French notification revoked lawfully, terms
renegotiated or later accession application; markets and sunk costs do not
automatically reset, membership reinstatement not a free toggle.

## AI and acceptance gates
EU Commission, Council/European Council, Parliament, ECB, member
governments and other countries have independent actors, legal mandates,
budget and balance-sheet constraints. Track attitudes and commitments
over time without assigning real future political probabilities. Response
choices include cooperation, negotiation, legal objection, changed
budget priorities, market stabilisation, domestic review or withdrawal
procedure. Reasons explain legal competence, financial constraints,
economic exposure, agreement compliance and domestic conditions.
Policy and economic feedback affects subsequent French options, including
Russia trade, energy network access, and financial terms. France's
departure changes neither foreign sanctions nor third-country permissions
automatically; the remaining EU may maintain or amend restrictions through
its own procedures. No evasion mechanics.

Acceptance: notification no automatic EU or monetary departure;
scope loss separate from fixed-cohort GDP growth; no double-counted
budget flows, TARGET positions or debt; no automatic legal cancellation
of contracts; Council/ECB weight denominators tested; no forced contagion;
foreign actors cannot be controlled by player; same seed/evidence yields
same results; trade/FX/spread sensitivities and stress paths include
positive and negative outcomes; legacy scenario replay unchanged.
WP48–50 economic foundations; WP61–69 institutions, AI, trade and exit;
WP74–76 data/calibration; WP77–80 long-run regression. Study only.
