# World Politics — French sovereign debt, creditor accounting and financial crisis paths

Reference 2026-11-25. France only playable initially. Design study for
WP49–50 and WP66–80, not implementation or forecast. Includes sovereign
default and contested ownership-transfer scenarios as abstract game
events, not a guide to taking real assets.

## Statistical anchors and stock-flow discipline
At 2026 Q2 Maastricht gross debt EUR 3,595.5bn; net 3,366.8bn; ratio 119%.
Consolidated contributions: state 2,942.1bn, other central government
69.2bn, local government 274.9bn, social security 309.4bn (rounded).
Instruments: deposits 42.7bn, securities 3,229.0bn, loans 323.8bn.
AFT 31 October 2026 state negotiable debt exact EUR 2,921,146,886,290;
not interchangeable with the general-government series.
Insee 30 October vintage 2025 Q4 3,460.1bn supersedes older 3,460.5bn
for this analysis, not for existing pinned worlds. 2025 increase 154.0bn;
2026 H1 135.4bn/181 days = ~748m/day; trailing four quarters +178.3bn.
Average is not a daily observation or a forecast. AFT September plan
2026 state financing need 311.7bn and budget debt charge 62.6bn,
2027 forecasts 339.7bn and 72.9bn. Do not confuse annual gross issuance,
net stock change, deficit, coupon accrual, redemptions or debt interest.

Implement dated daily journal in minor units with debt instrument IDs,
issuer/subsector, nominal vs market value, currency, principal, coupon,
indexation, maturity, accrued unpaid interest, governing law category,
holder-sector claims, status and verified payment events. Import public
AFT security schedule (ISIN, contractual terms, maturity) independently
from holder data. Daily/weekly/calendar-month/calendar-year and trailing
12 month aggregates: opening and closing stock, issuance, principal
paid, indexation, FX valuation, debt recognition/reclassification,
lawful extinguishment/restructuring, arrears, cash interest, accrual,
primary balance and treasury cash. Daily stock change =
new principal - principal extinguished + indexation/FX/other adjustments
with consolidation; missed principal stays outstanding; accrue unpaid
interest separately and capitalise only per legal terms. Refinancing
repays old debt and creates new debt, not twice the deficit. Track gross
financing need (maturities + cash deficit + other cash needs) and actual
subscriptions, cash shortfalls. No automatic unlimited borrowing.
Calibrate on actual coupon and maturity schedule, no uniform rate on
entire debt. Consolidated general government vs state financing separate.

## Creditors: coverage, not invented certainty
AFT/BdF 2026 Q2 market value: nonresidents 59.66% (59.7% rounded);
French credit institutions 10.5%, insurers 9.2%, funds 2.0%, other
French holders 18.7%; rounded chart totals 100.1%. Preserve precision
and rounding residual. Never multiply those market-value shares by an
October nominal amount as if exact. BdF APP/PEPP holdings of public
debt 488bn EUR amortised cost at June 30, 2026 (546bn end 2025).
May overlap holder categories, not an additional creditor total.
Residence, legal owner, ultimate beneficiary, custodian, currency,
governing law, asset location and jurisdiction are distinct. Intermediary
and underlying holder cannot both be counted as owners of same claim.
Public information cannot establish every beneficial holder of marketable
bonds; secondary trading changes holdings. IMF PIP/CPIS aggregate data,
ECB/BdF securities statistics, EBA sovereign exposure disclosures
can improve geographic/sector coverage. Do not equate sovereign exposure
with a verified individual holding, or missing with zero.
Complete accounting in game: observed aggregate buckets plus explicitly
fictional residual creditors generated deterministically, never label
synthetic allocation as actual named institution holdings. Privacy and
licensing controls. Each claim maps to exactly one final accounting
holder at each tick, reconciles to instrument outstanding. No real-world
target lists or asset inventories for seizure.

## Player decisions and ownership/legal model
Independent options: normal repayment, negotiated reprofiling,
voluntary exchange, coupon reduction, haircut, moratorium, selective
default, broad default, contested repudiation, nationalisation,
court-ordered seizure, freezing and other abstract ownership events.
All are distinct legal/economic events. Formal constitutional process
for company nationalisation (Article 34), property protection and just
prior indemnity under Article 17 DDHC; human-rights/treaty review,
independent courts and possible compensation claims. A fictional regime
breach may occur through the institutional/crisis system, with
contestability, reactions, and explicit status, not assumed lawful or
automatically successful. No practical instructions or maps for
confiscating real creditor assets. Model abstract fictional asset
categories with jurisdiction, ownership, third-party rights, immunity,
valuation, liabilities, compensation, court status and operational
control. State can own a company without owning deposits held by it.
Nationalisation changes shareholder ownership, not creditors' claims,
does not magically turn illiquid assets into Treasury cash or extinguish
debt; acquisition/compensation may require financing. A freeze restricts
disposal, not ownership. Seizure/transfer changes ownership only upon
resolved event; contested status records claim and restitution risk.
Separate domestic reach from legal permission; foreign court rulings,
treaty claims and diplomatic effects independent. Never infer reach
from nationality or residence. Model indirect effects on savers, bank
depositors, pension/insurance customers and counterparties.

## Shock paths and international support
Compare same initial state and external shocks, maintenance of payments,
negotiated pre-default exchange, partial/default after missed payments,
contested repudiation, and combinations with ownership changes,
EU/euro exit durations, Russia normalisation and no exit. No automatic
game over. A default changes contract performance, market valuation,
eligibility, refinancing offers and confidence; economic transmission
through bank capital, collateral, liquidity, credit, investment, imports,
FX, CPI, unemployment, revenues and public services. Mark-to-market
losses and realised losses separate, no double counting, recovery rates
and time to restructure variable. Nationalisations may maintain services
or impose costs; track compensation, operation and liabilities.
Assess cross-border spillovers, foreign creditor country losses,
EU/ECB/ESM decisions, potential litigation and diplomatic or trade
responses; no automatic sanction or euro collapse. IMF 2019 finds
different banking/output channels for preemptive vs post-default
restructurings; no direct transplant of historical coefficients to France.
Support via agreed bilateral/multilateral loans, equity investment,
grants, guarantees, trade credits and legally available central bank
liquidity, with currency, maturity, conditions, collateral, available
resources, legislative approval and disbursement. Allies can decline,
delay, negotiate, condition or terminate; promises are not cash.
Loan = new liability; guarantee not immediate receipt; foreign currency
requires FX accounting; no duplicated support or guaranteed success.
Article 123 TFEU restricts direct monetary financing while applicable.
Track partner balance sheet impact and own interests, not friendship
score alone. Responses can range from stabilisation to severe crisis,
with independent domestic and international decisions. Test no allied
support, partial/full lawful committed support, delayed support and
withdrawal; vary recovery, funding spread, exchange rate and demand
at 1/3/6/12/24/36/60/120 months. No probability assigned without data.

## Acceptance
Every cent reconciles opening and closing stocks/flows in game; unknown
real-world ownership never fabricated; no automatic extinguishment at
default or nationalisation; independent legal states, financing caps,
foreign actors and third-party obligations; all historical measures
retain date, valuation and vintage; deterministic replay, no cross-world
contamination. Add aggregate asset classes and fictitious holder
registry, no real creditor seizure target database. Existing WP48
model uses assumed automatic financing; replace in new scenario only.
All actions interact with existing EU/euro and Russia studies. WP80
remains target.
