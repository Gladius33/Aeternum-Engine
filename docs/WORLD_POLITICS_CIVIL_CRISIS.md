Count definitions differ; organisers can publish different estimates. These are
not caps or real-time operational information. Simulation uses committed
population and dissatisfaction with illustrative city weights and an illustrative
uncertainty interval; no individual tracking, forecast, security force ratio,
unit placement, tactical logistics or weapon inventory. Public-service
continuity is separate, driven by aggregate funding. No assumption that
peaceful assembly is violence, and no causal path from crowd size to loss of
government or coercive success. A later civilian-service model can account for
fatigue, routine obligations and abstract resource limits independently.

Continuity events are trusted narrative events only: domestic relocation,
abroad and return. Physical absence does not imply constitutional incapacity,
vacancy or recognition of an exiled government. Existing presidential
calendar and certified terminal events still apply; no new extraordinary
authority, military action, tactical evacuation or restoration mechanics.
