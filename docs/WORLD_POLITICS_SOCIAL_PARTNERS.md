# WP53 — social partners

Opt-in scenario fr-social-partners-v1 inherits WP52 macro, debt, demography and
services. Five fictional representative bodies distinguish general employees,
health, education, employers and retirees. Daily observations derive only from
committed domestic snapshots (funding, unmet demand, unpaid pensions, unemployment)
and existing cohort pressure. Bounded grievance, sustained stress, consultation,
dialogue, unresolved disagreement and sustained relief are deterministic.
All thresholds and coefficients are illustrative, not official French statistics.

The state is pinned at bootstrap and journaled under the existing opinion checkpoint.
Old scenarios have no additional observation or state. The member-only read endpoint
GET /worlds/{world}/countries/FR/social-partners requires coherent clock state.
No direct write endpoint, no duplicate service expenditure or automatic economic
feedback, no legal bargaining mandate, actual named unions, signed agreements,
or automatic change of regime. Negotiation actions and certified agreements are
future gameplay work. Tests cover sector-specific stress, recovery, source ratios,
replay, tampering, membership and legacy isolation.
