# World Politics — route to a playable V1 (WP126–134)

Status: execution started, 28 November 2026. This supersedes no historical evidence or previously pinned rules. A technical private alpha is not a playable release. Work remains on the dedicated World Politics branch; no merge to generic engine main. The work packages below group unresolved items from WP114–125 and subsequent corrective increments.

## Release gates and scope

- **G0 — qualified baseline:** exact-head CI passes all game and Research jobs, verified archive, schema install/restore, previous save compatibility, no known critical runtime regression.
- **G1 — playable French private beta:** authentic join, fictional character or clearly identified provisional real profile, meaningful political actions from opposition through government, lawful decisions and economic consequences, AI continuation, recurring elections and a coherent score. A human can complete the loop using a responsive browser UI without operator-only commands for normal play.
- **G2 — international playable release:** reviewed national institutions and country-specific economic/diplomatic effects for the agreed playable roster (EU member states, Switzerland and the agreed major powers), common multi-country universes, country-role-specific permissions, AI-controlled unclaimed/nonplayable actors. Documentary-only entries never imply playable countries. Israel, Palestine/Palestinian Authority and Iran remain represented nonplayable actors per project scope. Final roster reviewed against product requirements before release.
- **G3 — public V1:** target-environment installation, security and load qualification, acceptance of the multiplayer and score contract, updated source editions and operational runbook. Experimental score cohorts and modeled economic uncertainty are labeled. Research scientific qualification and actual Stripe payment-provider acceptance are separate gates before advertising those services as validated.

A French beta is a milestone, not a substitute for G2/G3. Final 2027 campaign programmes and election results cannot be assumed before publication; preserve dated provisional editions and fictional alternatives. No arbitrary historical promises attributed to 2027. No forced action in an office not held by the player.

### Bicameral career pilot (new public edition v5)
WP127 adds an independent indirect-election Senate fixture with 348 numbered synthetic seats, a six-year term and alternate series of 170/178 seats every three years, starting with series 1 in 2029. Source: https://www.senat.fr/connaitre-le-senat/role-et-fonctionnement/mode-delection-des-senateurs.html. The September 30 handover and seven-day candidacy deadline are explicit game conventions. The electoral-college support weights are deterministic abstractions, not actual department/list outcomes; legal age/nationality eligibility is not certified. The clock settles signed partial renewals without expiring the other series. A member can relinquish their own active deputy/senator mandate to pursue another office; the old mandate and its voting authority remain closed. The UI and REST expose both functions, with idempotent receipts, membership checks and regression fixtures. Existing v4 worlds preserve their rules and cohort; newly created public worlds pin v5. Full legislative campaigning, Senate departmental/list allocation, dissolution transitions, economic calibration, shared multinational careers and browser acceptance remain open.


### Fictional electoral reputation (new public edition v6)
WP128 introduces signed political activities available even without an office: programme review, fictional meeting, debate and constituency work. One action per seven simulation days; repeated activity within 28 days reduces its effect, debate has deterministic adverse outcomes, and effects decay linearly over 180 days. The total modifier is bounded to -10%/+25% relative support weight (not percentage points of actual votes). Only the player's own authenticated career can act; the client cannot submit a popularity value, target voter attributes or choose a random seed. Presidential first-round, Assembly and Senate synthetic support weights consume only actions preceding the relevant cutoff. Opening ballots, previous editions and the economic score formula are unchanged. New universes receive a distinct cohort. The UI displays activities, effects and cooldown. This is a deliberately limited game mechanic, not a real polling/behavioural model; coalition bargaining, full constitutional processes, cross-country economies and actual browser acceptance remain outstanding.
