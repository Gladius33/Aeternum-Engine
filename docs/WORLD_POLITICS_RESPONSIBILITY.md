# WP41 — linked Article 49(3) pilot

Opt-in fr-responsibility-v1. Uses the existing FrenchConstitutionalCrisis
aggregate with 577 fictional Assembly members, one pinned ordinary session
and only ordinary government bills in the normal Assembly reading stage.
Certified UTC timestamps within game days preserve the exact 24-hour filing
and 48-hour minimum vote intervals (Constitution articles 49 and 50).
A tenth of members (58) must sign; a majority (289) must vote for censure.
The same ordinary text may be reused in the session; a distinct text cannot.
Prior Council deliberation and certified prime-minister procedural facts
are the local clerk's responsibility. No public vote or engagement route.
Existing CLI legislation.php certify consumes responsibility_engage,
responsibility_file, responsibility_vote and responsibility_appoint evidence.
Each event is scoped to the actual bill and exact text. Appointment requires
certified presentation of resignation. Automatic clock expiry is replayed
at the exact boundary. No vote totals are invented for deemed adoption.
Adoption by the Assembly is not a promulgated law: Senate/navette and final
publication continue under WP40. Successful censure rejects the bill and
requires cabinet resignation; it does not end the presidency. A new cabinet
does not revive the rejected bill. Opinion receives a modest fictional
transient response to engagement, cabinet status follows the verified
daily institutional snapshot, and no social or regime outcome is automatic.
No new database migration. The pilot does not yet integrate the WP28
deputy-level roster, spontaneous censure or confidence votes, session
renewal, finance bills, Article 7 vacancy restrictions, actual PM roster
appointment, calendar-certified sittings, or automatic certified votes.
These remain explicit follow-up tasks. Historical scenarios unchanged.
Source: https://qpc360.conseil-constitutionnel.fr/constitution-4-octobre-1958
