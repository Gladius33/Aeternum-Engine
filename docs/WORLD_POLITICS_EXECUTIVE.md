# WP45 — executive acts pilot

Opt-in fr-executive-v1. Constitution articles 13, 19, 21, 22, 37 and 38:
https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006071194/.
Separate PM decrees, Council decrees and ordinances. Reviewed immutable
fictional templates only. The incumbent active PM and competent portfolio
holder are verified at signature; Council acts require presidential
signature and certified deliberation, ordinances also Council of State
consultation and a published limited habilitation. No arbitrary law by
decree. Publication is distinct; ordinance effective on publication;
missed ratification filing deadline lapses the ordinance on the clock.
Timely filing does not imply ratification: express ratification law and
matching bill digest required. The pilot uses exclusive deadline days;
legal dates are supplied by the certified scenario clerk. Habilitation
and ratification law digests are clerk-certified, not yet derived from
the bicameral aggregate. No modification or judicial review of ordinances
or automatic legislative effects is implemented.

GET /worlds/{world}/countries/FR/executive returns reviewed templates and
state. Existing trusted legislation CLI certify accepts executive_* events
with bill_id null. No public mutation/certification. apply-decree world-id
act-id verifies the published regulatory text and exact future domestic
proposal before using the existing two-transaction registry and authorization.
Only administration-report (500 EUR/day for 10 days, fictional minor units)
is supported; the ordinance has no fiscal policy mapping. Older scenarios
unchanged. No schema migration. Actual budget authorization is a scenario
assumption and no new real appropriations are implied. Administrative
litigation, detailed countersignature rules, delegated authority,
automated legislative habilitation and player action workflows remain open.
